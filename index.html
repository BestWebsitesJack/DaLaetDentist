About DeLaet Dentistry

A Practice
Built on Community

DeLaet Dentistry may be a new name to some, but this practice has a long and successful history serving the White Oak community for over 45 years.

Our Story

A White Oak Staple Since 1979

DeLaet Dentistry continues the legacy of Dr. Ken Brandt, DDS, who served this community for 45 years before transitioning into a well-earned retirement. This practice has been a staple in the White Oak community for decades, proudly serving families across White Oak, Groesbeck, Monfort Heights, Cheviot, Dent, North Bend, and all of western Cincinnati.

The practice has undergone recent renovations with a complete upgrade of the operating space and technology to give our patients the best experience possible. We are thrilled for the future growth of our practice and can’t wait to share the journey with all our patients.

Our mission is simple: treat every patient as if they are an extension of our own family so that they feel comfortable and valued. The most rewarding moments are when patients leave our office with a completely confident and proud smile on their faces.

We strive to provide exceptional comprehensive dental care while building a positive patient-doctor relationship based on mutual trust, excellent customer service, and personalized care. Every patient and every case is different — and we treat them that way.

Meet Dr. DeLaet

Dr. Tyler (TJ) DeLaet, DMD

Dr. Tyler (TJ) DeLaet, DMD was born and raised in the White Oak area of Cincinnati, OH. Dr. DeLaet graduated from La Salle High School (2010) and began his collegiate studies at the University of Cincinnati where he graduated with his Bachelors Degree in Health Sciences (2014) and his Masters Degree in Biological Sciences (2015). Following his studies at the University of Cincinnati, Dr. DeLaet went onto the University of Louisville School of Dentistry where he graduated with his Doctorate of Dental Medicine Degree (DMD) in 2019.

Dr. DeLaet has always dreamed of owning his own dental practice in his hometown and serving the local community of White Oak. After graduating from dental school, Dr. DeLaet moved back to Cincinnati and began his dental career practicing in Harrison, OH. Eventually the opportunity presented itself for Dr. DeLaet to purchase this successful and well-respected dental practice from Dr. Ken Brandt, DDS. Dr. DeLaet is excited about the future development of this dental practice and is thrilled to be working back in his hometown.

Dr. DeLaet is a member of the American Dental Association. In his leisure time Dr. DeLaet enjoys playing golf, training local baseball players with his dad, and being an avid Cincinnati sports fan.

Preventive Care

Maintaining Your Best Smile

We are a general dentist office providing comprehensive, family, and cosmetic dentistry. We treat all aspects of dentistry and hope to educate our patients extensively on their oral health status and how they can help improve and maintain their own oral health.

We pride ourselves on being a family-oriented practice welcoming all demographics from pediatrics to geriatrics. We hope everyone feels comfortable and welcomed, addressing all your dental care needs.

Questions? Call Us →
Routine Cleanings & Exams
Prophylactic cleanings are recommended every 6 months with your dental hygienist, along with exams by Dr. DeLaet to monitor your oral health.
X-Rays & Radiographs
Routine intraoral radiographs recommended every 12 months. Panoramic radiographs every 3–5 years for a full view of the jaw, neck, and surrounding structures.
Deep Cleanings
Scaling and root planing for patients showing signs of periodontal (gum) disease, performed to restore gum health and prevent progression.
Fluoride Treatments
Fluoride application to strengthen enamel and help prevent cavities, especially beneficial for children and patients at higher risk.
Sealants
Protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to prevent decay in hard-to-reach grooves and pits.
Cosmetic Dentistry

Your Best Smile Awaits

Cosmetic dentistry at DeLaet Dentistry goes beyond aesthetics — it’s about giving you the confidence that comes with a smile you love. Dr. DeLaet works with each patient to understand their goals and develop a personalized treatment plan.

Questions? Call Us →
Teeth Whitening
Professional in-office and take-home whitening treatments to brighten your smile significantly more effectively than over-the-counter products.
Veneers
Thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct shape, size, color, and minor misalignment for a dramatically improved smile.
Cosmetic Bonding
Tooth-colored composite resin used to repair chips, cracks, gaps, or discoloration — a quick and cost-effective cosmetic solution.
Smile Makeovers
Comprehensive cosmetic treatment plans combining multiple procedures to achieve your ideal smile transformation.
Restorative Dentistry

Restoring Function & Beauty

When teeth are damaged, decayed, or missing, our restorative treatments bring back the function and appearance of your smile. We use the latest materials and techniques for natural-looking, long-lasting results.

Questions? Call Us →
Fillings
Tooth-colored composite fillings to restore teeth damaged by decay, matching the natural appearance of your tooth.
Crowns & Bridges
Porcelain crowns restore damaged or severely decayed teeth. Bridges replace missing teeth using adjacent teeth as anchors for a fixed, natural-looking restoration.
Implant Crown Restorations
Dr. DeLaet restores dental implants placed by a specialist with custom-crafted crowns for a permanent, natural-feeling tooth replacement.
Partial & Complete Dentures
Custom removable appliances to replace multiple missing teeth, restoring your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
Root Canal Therapy
When infection reaches the inner pulp of a tooth, root canal treatment removes the infected tissue, relieves pain, and saves the natural tooth.
Orthodontics

Straighter Smiles, Clearer Confidence

Invisalign clear aligner therapy offers a discreet, comfortable, and effective way to straighten teeth without the brackets and wires of traditional braces. Dr. DeLaet is trained in Invisalign treatment and uses our iTero 3D scanner to plan every case with precision.

Questions? Call Us →
Invisalign
A series of clear, removable aligners that gradually move teeth into their ideal positions. No brackets, no wires, and virtually invisible.
Occlusal Guards / Night Guards
Custom-fitted removable appliances for patients who grind or clench their teeth (bruxism), protecting enamel and reducing jaw discomfort.
Retainers
Following orthodontic treatment, retainers maintain the position of your teeth to ensure your new smile lasts a lifetime.

iTero 3D Digital Scanner

The Latest Advancement in Dental Care

Digital scanners have become the latest and greatest advancement in the dental field. This technology allows our clinical staff to fully educate patients on their personal dental status by creating 3D replicas of their teeth and taking photos at the same time. It eliminates the need for old-school impression methods when it comes to crown restorations, denture fabrication, occlusal guard fabrication, Invisalign fabrication, and so much more.

Precise 3D digital impressions — no messy molds or gag-inducing trays
Real-time visualization of your teeth during your appointment
Used for Invisalign planning, crowns, dentures, night guards, and more
Significantly more accurate than traditional impression methods
Speeds up lab turnaround for restorations
Digital X-Rays

Crystal Clear, Lower Radiation

Our office uses fully digital radiographic technology, replacing traditional film X-rays. Digital X-rays produce high-quality images with significantly less radiation exposure, and they can be displayed on-screen instantly so Dr. DeLaet can walk you through exactly what he sees.

Up to 90% less radiation compared to traditional film X-rays
Instant images available on screen during your appointment
Enhanced ability to zoom, adjust contrast, and identify issues
Bitewing X-rays recommended annually; panoramic every 3–5 years
Panoramic X-rays show full jaw, surrounding bone structure, and more
Digital Panoramic X-Ray

The Full Picture

A panoramic radiograph is a single full-mouth 2D image showing all aspects of the oral cavity as well as the surrounding structures and tissues of the skull and spine. Updated every 3–5 years, this image allows Dr. DeLaet to identify any abnormalities — cysts, tumors, and other concerns — and ensure the jaw and surrounding structures are in good health.

Full-mouth view in a single image
Identifies issues beyond what standard X-rays can show
Essential for evaluating jaw health, bone levels, and surrounding structures
Non-invasive and quick — takes only seconds to capture
Stored digitally for easy reference and comparison over time
Electronic Digital Charting

Paperless, Precise, Efficient

Previously, patient records were maintained on paper. DeLaet Dentistry has fully transitioned to electronic digital charting, allowing Dr. DeLaet and staff to review records quickly and effectively while also giving patients the ability to view their radiographs directly during treatment discussions.

Complete transition from paper to fully electronic records
Instant access to patient history, treatment notes, and radiographs
Patients can view their own X-rays on screen during appointments
Seamless coordination between doctor, hygienist, and front desk
Secure, HIPAA-compliant digital records storage
Intraoral Cameras

See What We See

Intraoral cameras are small, handheld devices that allow us to take detailed photos inside your mouth. These high-resolution images are displayed on a chair-side screen so you can see exactly what Dr. DeLaet sees — making it easier to understand your oral health status and feel confident in any treatment recommendations.

High-definition photos of teeth, gums, and oral tissues
Displayed on-screen in real time during your appointment
Helps patients understand their dental health visually
Useful for detecting cracks, decay, and gum issues early
Images stored in your digital chart for future reference

Insurance

We Work With Your Insurance

We accept most dental insurance plans — both in-network and out-of-network. Our goal is simple: help you get the most out of your benefits so cost is never a barrier to the care you need. If you have questions about your specific plan, just give us a call and we’ll walk you through it.

In-Network Plans

We are currently in-network with Delta Dental and Dental Care Plus. Being in-network means our office has a pre-established rate with your insurance provider, which typically results in the lowest possible out-of-pocket cost for you.

As an in-network patient, you have full access to all benefits provided by your plan at the contracted rate.

Out-of-Network Plans

Don’t see your insurance listed? No problem. We work with virtually all insurance companies and will submit your claims directly on your behalf as a courtesy — regardless of network status.

Out-of-network simply means there isn’t a pre-negotiated rate with your provider. Most plans still cover a significant portion of your care. We’ll give you a clear estimate before any treatment begins so there are never any surprises.

A Few Things Worth Knowing
Payment is due at the time of service. We provide a clear cost estimate before every treatment so you always know what to expect.
We submit all insurance claims on your behalf as a courtesy — in-network or out-of-network. Most claims are processed within 2–6 weeks.
Any outstanding balance after insurance payment is the patient’s responsibility. We’ll always make sure you understand this before treatment begins.
Please let us know of any changes to your insurance plan or provider so we can keep your file current and your benefits maximized.

Have questions about your specific plan? Call our office and we’ll be happy to verify your benefits before your visit.
