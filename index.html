01 — Our Story

A White Oak staple
since 1979.

DeLaet Dentistry continues the legacy of Dr. Ken Brandt, DDS — who served this community for 45 years before transitioning into a well-earned retirement.

This practice has been a staple in the White Oak community for decades, proudly serving families across White Oak, Groesbeck, Monfort Heights, Cheviot, Dent, North Bend, and all of western Cincinnati.

The practice has undergone recent renovations with a complete upgrade of the operating space and technology to give our patients the best experience possible. We are thrilled for the future growth of our practice and can’t wait to share the journey with all our patients.

Our mission is simple: treat every patient as if they are an extension of our own family — so that they feel comfortable and valued. The most rewarding moments are when patients leave our office with a completely confident and proud smile on their faces.

We strive to provide exceptional dental care while building a positive patient-doctor relationship based on mutual trust, excellent customer service, and personalized care.
Dr. Tyler DeLaet, DMD DeLaet Dentistry
02 — Meet Dr. DeLaet

Dr. Tyler (TJ)
DeLaet, DMD

Dr. Tyler (TJ) DeLaet, DMD was born and raised in the White Oak area of Cincinnati. He graduated from La Salle High School (2010) and began his collegiate studies at the University of Cincinnati — earning his B.S. in Health Sciences (2014) and M.S. in Biological Sciences (2015).

Following his studies at the University of Cincinnati, Dr. DeLaet went on to the University of Louisville School of Dentistry, where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) in 2019.

Dr. DeLaet has always dreamed of owning his own dental practice in his hometown and serving the local community of White Oak. After graduating from dental school, he moved back to Cincinnati and began his dental career practicing in Harrison, OH. Eventually the opportunity presented itself to purchase this successful and well-respected practice from Dr. Ken Brandt, DDS.

Dr. DeLaet is a member of the American Dental Association. In his leisure time he enjoys playing golf, training local baseball players with his dad, and being an avid Cincinnati sports fan.

01 — Preventive

Maintaining
your best smile.

The best dental work is the kind you never need. We focus on prevention so small issues stay small — and your smile stays yours.

What’s included: Routine cleanings, oral exams, digital X-rays, oral cancer screenings, fluoride treatments, sealants.

Modern digital X-rays mean less radiation and clearer images. Combined with our iTero 3D scanner, we see things traditional checkups miss.

02 — Cosmetic

Your best smile
awaits.

Cosmetic dentistry is about more than teeth — it’s about confidence. The way you smile in photos. The way you feel in a job interview. The way you catch yourself in the mirror.

What’s included: Teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, cosmetic bonding, full smile makeovers.

Every cosmetic case begins with a conversation. We want to understand what you’re hoping for before we discuss what’s possible.

03 — Restorative

Restoring
function & beauty.

When teeth are damaged, broken, or missing, we rebuild them — so you can eat, speak, and smile without a second thought.

What’s included: Fillings, crowns, bridges, partial and full dentures, root canal therapy, extractions.

We use materials that match your natural tooth color and techniques that minimize discomfort. Modern restorative dentistry is almost invisible.

04 — Orthodontics

A straighter smile,
on your terms.

Invisalign gives you the smile you want without a mouth full of metal. Clear aligners, custom-made, worn on your schedule.

What’s included: Full Invisalign treatment planning, aligner fabrication and fittings, progress monitoring, retainers.

Dr. DeLaet is a Certified Invisalign Provider. Treatment typically takes 6–18 months depending on your case.

05 — Family

Care for
every stage of life.

We take care of entire families — your kids, your parents, your grandparents. One practice. Every generation.

What’s included: Pediatric dentistry, teen Invisalign, adult preventive and restorative care, senior dental care.

Kids’ first visits are gentle and friendly. We take the time to make the dentist feel like a safe place — because it is.
